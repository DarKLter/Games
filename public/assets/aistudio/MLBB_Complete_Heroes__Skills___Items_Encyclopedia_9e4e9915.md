## Part 1: Core Hero & Combat Mechanics Overview

### 1. The 6 Hero Roles & 5 Battlefield Lanes
* **Tank (Roam / EXP):** High HP, Physical/Magic Defense, and Crowd Control (CC) initiators who absorb enemy burst, provide bush vision, and protect carries.
* **Fighter (EXP / Jungle / Roam):** Balanced melee bruisers combining sustained damage, spell vamp/lifesteal sustain, and frontline durability.
* **Assassin (Jungle / Mid / Roam):** High-mobility burst specialists designed to flank backlines, eliminate enemy Marksmen/Mages, and secure Turtle/Lord with Retribution.
* **Mage (Mid / Gold / Roam):** High Magic Damage casters specializing in area-of-effect (AoE) burst, wave clear, zone control, and crowd control.
* **Marksman (Gold / Jungle):** Ranged physical DPS carries who scale exponentially with items to shred turrets, tanks, and bosses in the mid-to-late game.
* **Support (Roam / Mid):** Utility specialists who heal allies, grant shields, boost team movement speed, cleanse debuffs, or lock down priority targets.

### 2. Crowd Control (CC) Priority Hierarchy (Lowest to Highest)
1. **Slow:** Reduces Movement Speed (and occasionally Attack Speed).
2. **Immobilize / Root:** Prevents walking and dash/blink skills; the hero can still cast non-movement skills and Basic Attack.
3. **Silence / Disarm:** Silence blocks skill casts; Disarm blocks Basic Attacks.
4. **Stun / Freeze / Petrify / Taunt / Terrify / Transform:** Full action lock (can be removed by **Purify** or CC-cleansing skills).
5. **Airborne / Knockback / Pull:** Forced physical displacement plus full action lock (interrupts channeled abilities).
6. **Suppression:** Highest CC tier (e.g., Franco, Kaja, Barats)—bypasses CC Immunity and **cannot** be removed by Purify.
