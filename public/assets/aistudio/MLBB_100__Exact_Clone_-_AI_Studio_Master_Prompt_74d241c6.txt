5.1 EXACT MLBB COMBAT FORMULAS:
- Physical & Magic Damage Reduction Formula:
  `EffectiveDefense = (TargetDefense - FlatPenetration) * (1 - PercentPenetration)` (Can go negative down to `-60` for amplified damage!)
  `If EffectiveDefense >= 0: DamageMultiplier = 120 / (120 + EffectiveDefense)`
  `If EffectiveDefense < 0: DamageMultiplier = 2 - (120 / (120 - EffectiveDefense))`
- True Damage: Ignores all Defense and Damage Reduction (still blocked by Shields unless specifically bypassing).
- Critical Strike: Base 200% Physical Damage (240% with Berserker's Fury).
- Caps & Soft Caps:
  * Cooldown Reduction (CDR): Hard cap 40% (45% with Enchanted Talisman).
  * Attack Speed: Hard cap 3.00 attacks/sec (5.00 with Golden Staff/uncapped heroes).
  * Movement Speed Soft Caps: Full value up to 415; between 415 and 490 gains 80% efficiency; above 490 gains 50% efficiency.
- Healing & Anti-Heal: Lifesteal (Basic Attacks), Spell Vamp (Skills), Hybrid Lifesteal (Both). Anti-Heal items (Sea Halberd, Glowing Wand, Dominance Ice) reduce enemy Shields & HP Regen by 50% for 3s.
